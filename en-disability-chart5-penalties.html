Penalties in the new draft law for abusing disability vehicle exemptions

Forging the card: up to 10 years · Unlawful exemption: 6 months + up to 200,000 EGP

“The draft raises the penalty for forging a disability card to imprisonment not exceeding ten years and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 pounds, and punishes unlawful exemptions with no less than six months imprisonment and a fine of 50,000 to 200,000 pounds.”
